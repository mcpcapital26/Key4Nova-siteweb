Who we are

About Key4Nova.

Based in Rhode-Saint-Genèse, we connect employers and candidates through specialist recruitment in Life Sciences, Healthcare and industry across French- and Dutch-speaking Belgium.

  • Useful innovation
  • Accountability
  • Agility
  • Transparency
Our expertise

From specialists to executives, we know the work and the people behind it.

Our team recruits across life sciences, healthcare, medical devices, biotechnology and industry. Each search is shaped around the role, the organisation and the candidate’s ambitions.

How we work

Direct in conversation. Thoughtful in our decisions.

We say what we do and assign a clear point of contact to each search. Tools help us research and assess with care; conversations and human judgment remain central to every hire.

Our local roots and the international INRALS network allow us to support searches in Belgium and beyond when the assignment calls for it.
