How we help

The right search for the right role.

From specialists to senior leaders, we adapt the search to your responsibilities, market and organisation.

EXECUTIVE SEARCH

Executive Search

For leadership and strategic appointments, we define the brief with you, map the market and approach relevant people directly, including those who are not actively looking. Discretion and fit with the organisation guide the search.

SPECIALIST RECRUITMENT

Specialist profiles

We recruit scientific, technical, commercial and operational talent in Life Sciences, Healthcare and industry. Beyond a CV, we assess skills, motivation and the setting in which a person can thrive.

ASSESSMENT

Assessment Center

Structured interviews and exercises relevant to the role help clarify skills, potential and development needs. You gain practical evidence to support hiring and onboarding decisions.

FLEXIBLE EXPERTISE

Key4Nova Flex

Key4Nova Flex finds professionals for replacements, defined projects and leadership transitions through specialist interim staffing, consulting or interim management. Explore Flex ↗
